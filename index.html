Scholarships

Scholarships provide financial support to help students pay for their education. They may cover part or all of tuition, living expenses, books, travel, or other educational costs. Many scholarships are based on academic achievement, financial need, leadership, field of study, or other eligibility requirements.

Fellowships are structured programs that support individuals through learning, research, leadership, professional development, or community-focused work. Depending on the program, fellows may receive funding, training, mentorship, networking, or other forms of support.
