OUR PURPOSE

One place.
More opportunities.

Finding the right opportunity can take a lot of time.

Scholarships may be posted on one website, internships on another, job openings somewhere else, and fellowship or grant opportunities may be spread across many different platforms.

Opportunity Platform brings these opportunities together in one place, making it easier to discover options that may be relevant to you.

Instead of searching through countless websites, you can use one platform to explore opportunities and find the official application source.

A Note on Transparency

Opportunity Platform is an aggregator, not a provider. We do not offer scholarships, jobs, internships, fellowships, or grants ourselves. All opportunities listed on our platform are provided by external organizations such as universities, companies, NGOs, government agencies, and funding bodies.

We provide links to official application sources and summarize key details to help you decide what to apply for. Always verify eligibility requirements and deadlines on the official organization's website before applying.

If you find an expired listing, incorrect information, or have concerns about any opportunity, please contact us so we can investigate and update our records.
