Frequently asked questions

Can I apply for an opportunity through this platform?

No. Opportunity Platform helps you discover opportunities and provides links to the official application pages. You complete your application directly with the organization offering the opportunity.

Can I submit an opportunity to be listed?

Yes. If you know about a legitimate scholarship, job, internship, fellowship, or grant that may be useful to our users, contact us with the relevant information and official source.

What should I do if I find an expired opportunity?

Please let us know. Include the opportunity title and the relevant link if possible so we can review the listing and update or remove it.

Do you charge users to access opportunities?

No. The platform is designed to make opportunity discovery accessible without charging users to browse opportunity information.

Looking for an opportunity?

You may not need to contact us at all. Start exploring scholarships, jobs, internships, fellowships, and grants.
